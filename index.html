Welcome to Paws of Hope

Paws of Hope Animal Rescue is a community-focused animal rescue organisation dedicated to helping abandoned, neglected and vulnerable animals.

Our mission is to provide animals with care, protection and a second chance at finding a loving home.

Explore our adoption and rescue services

Our Mission

We rescue animals in need, provide temporary care and work to connect them with responsible and loving families.

What We Do

  • Rescue abandoned and vulnerable animals.
  • Provide temporary shelter and care.
  • Promote responsible pet ownership.
  • Help animals find suitable forever homes.

How You Can Help

  1. Learn about animals available for adoption.
  2. Complete an adoption enquiry.
  3. Prepare your home for a new pet.

Every small action can make a difference and help an animal find a better future.
